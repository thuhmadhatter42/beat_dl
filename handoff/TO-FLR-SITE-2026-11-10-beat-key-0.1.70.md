## Page text that is now wrong (please check /beat-key and correct what applies)

1. Anything saying Listen hears only the player apps (Spotify, Music, a browser) or leaves out
   other apps. From 0.1.60 Listen hears every app on the Mac except Beat Key itself. To keep a
   microphone out of the reading, choose an input in Settings.
2. Anything about clicking a key name to switch between sharp and flat. That click is gone
   (0.1.65). Key names are always Auto-Tune's spelling.
3. Anything about the notice "NO SOUND FROM PLAYERS · HEARING ALL APPS". It no longer exists. The
   one notice is "NO SOUND · CHECK SOURCE", shown when the reading restarts because of quiet.
